Loquibot

Thank you everyone for using loquibot! Unfortunately loquibot has shut down, here are some alternatives you can try.

Alternatives

These are not affiliated with Loquibot and are entirely separate tools.

gdreqbot

A simple Twitch chatbot for managing Geometry Dash level request streams.
Early Development, chatbot only.

Formula (Geode)

The Formula request system, a project aimed to making level requests as streamlined as possible.

Google Form Requests (Geode)

Do level requests directly in-game by linking to a Google Spreadsheet connected to a Google Form.
